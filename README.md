# Kidds-Technical-Services

Kidd’s Technical Services

Kidd’s Technical Services develops and operates locally governed digital infrastructure for maps, heritage records, public information, and organization-specific knowledge.

KTS digitizes and organizes approved map collections and place-based records, loads them into ALLIS nodes, and provides locally governed web, kiosk, Wi‑Fi, and offline-capable access. Collection stewards retain ownership and control; KTS provides the technical implementation, managed operation, maintenance, support, and exportable preservation package.

KTS works with municipalities, archives, libraries, universities, heritage organizations, tourism partners, businesses, and public/private sponsors. Harmony for Hope may sponsor qualifying community-benefit projects, but KTS services do not require Harmony for Hope involvement.
