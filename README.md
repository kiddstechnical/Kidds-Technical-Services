# Kidd’s Technical Services

Kidd’s Technical Services, LLC (KTS) is a technical-services organization providing locally grounded mapping, public-information, digital-preservation, web-connected, archive, heritage, training, and managed-support services.

KTS helps organizations organize approved information, make it more usable, preserve it responsibly, and provide practical access through maps, websites, directories, public-information tools, training, and ongoing technical care.

## What KTS Does

KTS provides defined professional services for organizations that need clearer, more durable, and more useful access to place-based information, records, collections, and public-facing resources.

Current service areas include:

- Digital heritage, archive, genealogy, cemetery, and place-based record discovery and preservation.
- Mapping, resource directories, visitor information, local-information tools, and public-information systems.
- Data preparation, organization, documentation, and visualization.
- Website-connected maps, location pages, QR-linked information hubs, and practical digital tools.
- Support for municipalities, libraries, museums, archives, historical societies, nonprofits, tourism organizations, businesses, and professional partners.
- Partner-firm subcontracting for mapping, research, data, web-connected, archive, and technical-production work.
- Managed support, update services, hosting coordination, documentation, and training.

KTS works through written scopes, approved data access, defined deliverables, documented payment terms, and appropriate customer acceptance processes.

KTS uses written scopes and payment controls to protect customer delivery, contractor payments, and operating capacity.

```text
Customer deposit, milestone payment, recurring service revenue,
authorized funding, or lawful business capital
        ↓
KTS operating account
        ↓
Authorized delivery, payroll, contractor invoices,
software, insurance, equipment, marketing, and operations
        ↓
```

## Operating Principles

KTS uses the following principles in its work:

- **Collection stewards retain control.** Customers and lawful collection stewards retain ownership and decision-making authority over their records, materials, and approved uses.
- **Work must be authorized.** KTS uses written scopes, agreements, payment terms, permissions, and acceptance criteria appropriate to the work.
- **Data requires care.** KTS evaluates privacy, security, licensing, sensitivity, access, retention, and publication before handling or releasing information.
- **Pricing must support delivery.** KTS prices services to cover direct project costs and contribute to sustainable operating capacity.
- **Customer funds and KTS funds remain separate.** KTS maintains separate business records and does not mix customer, donor, nonprofit, contractor, grant, or personal funds without written authorization.
- **Marketing claims must be accurate.** KTS does not present planning assumptions, future concepts, unverified outcomes, or unready offerings as completed or available services.
- **Growth follows collected work.** KTS builds staffing and contractor capacity through funded work, deposits, milestone payments, recurring revenue, working capital, and documented margins.
