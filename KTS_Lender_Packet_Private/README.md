# Private lender packet

This directory is a public-safe placeholder only.

Do not store personal financial statements, tax returns, bank statements,
Medicaid-waiver caregiver-income documents, credit reports, customer records,
legal engagement records, passwords, or other confidential information in this repository.

Maintain actual lender materials in a secure, private location and share them
only with authorized advisors, lenders, accountants, insurers, or counsel.
