## Purpose

This notice identifies the source record for FTIS-001 and explains how Kidd's Technical Services will treat founder technical-investment hours and any associated dollar valuation.

This notice is intended to prevent a reader from confusing documented founder labor with revenue, cash, debt, grant match, company value, or an independently verified appraisal.

## Controlling source record

The controlling source record for the individual work entries supporting FTIS-001 is the KTS detailed technical-work ledger for the covered period.

FTIS-001 summarizes the ledger. If the summary and the detailed ledger differ, the detailed ledger controls unless KTS issues a dated correction to the ledger or FTIS-001.

KTS will retain the detailed ledger and supporting technical documentation as controlled evidence. KTS may provide relevant portions to authorized reviewers, including legal counsel, accountants, lenders, funders, government agencies, and due-diligence reviewers, subject to confidentiality, scope, and data-protection requirements.

## Why no dollar total appears in FTIS-001

KTS records the hours as a factual work record. A dollar value is not a single universal fact because the appropriate treatment depends on the purpose for which a reader proposes to use it.

Potential uses can include:

- Founder-capital or business-formation documentation.
- A contract, statement of work, or change-order cost basis.
- A grant in-kind-match analysis.
- A financing, lending, or business-plan submission.
- Internal accounting or tax review.
- Intellectual-property provenance and due diligence.

Each use can require a different rate, methodology, date, approval path, or eligibility analysis. KTS will not present one valuation rate as universally binding across all of these contexts.

## Known historical rate references

Related controlled documentation has referenced more than one hourly rate for the same underlying founder-work record. These references arose in different contexts and are not interchangeable.

| Founder's EMS Vesting Schedule | $300 per hour | Fair-market reference in a founder/EMS governance context | Use only if the applicable governance instrument, valuation basis, and approval process support that rate |

Neither historical reference automatically establishes:

- The fair-market value of KTS.
- The value of ALLIS intellectual property.
- A lender-approved or funder-approved contribution amount.
- An accounting or tax basis.
- An obligation for any organization to pay KTS or the founder.
- A rate applicable to future customer work.

## Required valuation statement

Any KTS document that assigns a dollar value to FTIS-001 hours must state all of the following:

1. The specific purpose of the valuation.
2. The number of hours being valued.
3. The rate or methodology used.
4. The effective date of the rate or methodology.
5. The source or approval basis for the rate or methodology.
6. Whether the amount is cash, contributed services, an internal planning figure, an in-kind-match estimate, or another category.
7. Any limitations on eligibility, reliance, or approval.

Use this model language:

> For the limited purpose of [purpose], KTS values [number] documented founder technical-investment hours at [rate or methodology], effective [date], based on [approval or source]. This amount is [classification] and is subject to [applicable agreement, funder rule, accounting treatment, or reviewer approval]. It does not establish a general company valuation, cash contribution, accounts receivable, debt obligation, or guaranteed eligible match.

## Use by external reviewers

An external reviewer may cite FTIS-001 to understand the scale and documented history of the founder's technical contribution. A reviewer must independently determine whether any dollar value is acceptable for its own decision.

- **Funders** determine whether contributed services are eligible match under their written rules.
- **Lenders and investors** determine whether founder labor is relevant to underwriting, capitalization, collateral, or valuation.
- **Accountants and tax professionals** determine the proper accounting and tax treatment.
- **Legal counsel** determines whether a proposed founder-capital, license, related-party, or other transaction is properly documented.
- **Customers and partners** rely only on the terms of their executed agreement with KTS.

## Relationship to Harmony for Hope

KTS and Harmony for Hope, Inc. are separate organizations. Where the founder's technical work intersects with a Harmony for Hope project, any use of FTIS-001 hours must follow the applicable written service agreement, statement of work, conflict-of-interest procedure, funder rule, and required approval.

FTIS-001 and this notice do not authorize a payment, reimbursement, grant match, sponsorship, license, capital instrument, transfer of assets, or other related-party transaction.
