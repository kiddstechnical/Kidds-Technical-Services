## 1. Purpose

This statement records the founder's documented non-cash technical investment in the systems, documentation, architecture, and intellectual-property foundation associated with the Artificial Learning & Location Intelligence System (ALLIS) and related Kidd's Technical Services work.

KTS maintains this statement to support intellectual-property provenance, business planning, due diligence, and review of founder-contributed work. It is not an invoice, a payroll record, a request for payment, a cash-balance statement, or an independently audited business valuation.

## 2. Scope of founder contribution

The documented work covers the design, development, testing, documentation, and governance preparation of KTS technical systems and related foundational materials. Work categories include:

- Local AI and language-model orchestration.
- Geospatial data systems, mapping, and location intelligence.
- Data and knowledge infrastructure.
- System architecture, containerization, service integration, and deployment preparation.
- Authentication, access control, trust, verification, logging, and audit design.
- Privacy, governance, licensing, service-design, and related-party boundary documentation.
- Documentation, technical evidence, prototype validation, and commercialization preparation.
- Community-serving and public-benefit technical planning performed under applicable KTS/Harmony for Hope documentation.

The detailed technical-work ledger remains the source record for dates, descriptions, and hours. This statement summarizes that record; it does not replace it.
