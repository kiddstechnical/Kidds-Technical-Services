# Use of Funds

## Purpose

This document describes how Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS) plans to use startup and working capital.

The purpose of the funding is to establish and operate a durable technical-services organization that can:

- Pay qualified contributors for authorized work.
- Deliver contracted mapping, local-information, web-connected, digital-preservation, archive, heritage, managed-service, and training work.
- Maintain the software, insurance, equipment, administration, and customer-support systems needed to deliver responsibly.
- Protect cash flow when contractor invoices and operating costs come due before all customer payments are collected.
- Build a documented, financially disciplined path from paid early projects to repeatable services and recurring revenue.

This document is a planning model. It is not a loan application, loan approval, investment solicitation, securities offering, grant award, legal opinion, accounting opinion, tax opinion, or guarantee of future revenue.

KTS will revise this document after it receives actual contracts, invoices, deposits, customer-payment history, contractor agreements, payroll quotes, insurance quotes, financing terms, legal advice, accounting advice, and vendor pricing.

## Bootstrap Launch Boundary

The $700,000–$750,000 financial model is a **future full-capacity scenario**, not an immediate funding request, current operating budget, current payroll plan, or representation of current revenue.

It illustrates what KTS could require only after it has demonstrated customer demand, completed paid work, established reliable operating controls, documented actual costs, developed a credible revenue pipeline, and identified a justified path to larger-scale delivery capacity.

The immediate KTS path is a lean, staged bootstrap launch:

| Stage | Capital level | Immediate purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| **Stage 1: Lean launch** | $25,000 | Establish minimum operating readiness, validate customer demand, conduct outreach, issue proposals, collect deposits, deliver early paid projects, and protect controlled working capital. |
| **Stage 2: Evidence-based expansion** | Up to an additional $25,000 | Expand only after KTS documents paid work, customer demand, reliable delivery, cash-flow needs, actual operating costs, and a justified use of additional capital. |
| **Potential staged total** | Up to $50,000 | A possible bootstrap capitalization path, not a request to spend $50,000 immediately. |

KTS will use existing capability first, including its existing web presence, available business-email capacity where appropriately authorized, QGIS, low-cost operating tools, and project-based delivery methods.

KTS will not assume immediate large payroll, standing contractor commitments, major equipment purchases, office costs, large recurring software costs, ALLIS revenue, ALLIS product commercialization, or other speculative revenue as part of the lean launch.

During the bootstrap stage, KTS will prioritize small, fixed-price, customer-funded services such as verified local business information, trusted information hubs, customer-access pages, QR-linked information, and local access maps. KTS will seek written customer approval and required deposits before beginning material work.

The $700,000–$750,000 scenario should be revisited only after KTS has evidence supporting expansion, including:

- Paid customer projects, collected deposits, and final payments
- Documented direct project costs, delivery time, operating costs, and margins
- A credible qualified pipeline, recurring-service opportunity, or signed customer work
- Reliable bookkeeping, payment, proposal, approval, and project-record systems
- A defined need for additional equipment, insurance, storage, security, outreach capacity, project-funded contractors, or staffing
- A cash-flow and use-of-funds plan based on actual results rather than planning assumptions
- Any required funder, lender, partner, legal, insurance, tax, or compliance review

No amount in the future full-capacity scenario is a commitment to spend, hire, borrow, raise, purchase, deploy, or guarantee revenue. KTS will update financial projections and use-of-funds assumptions as actual customer results, verified costs, funding terms, and operating requirements become known.

## Funding Boundary

### ALLIS Boundary

ALLIS is excluded from this use-of-funds plan.

KTS will not use prospective ALLIS revenue, future ALLIS commercialization, future ALLIS customer adoption, or potential ALLIS platform value to justify this funding request, contractor commitments, payroll commitments, debt repayment, or business valuation.

KTS may separately conduct internal ALLIS research, readiness work, governance planning, documentation, and future commercialization planning. Those activities must remain separately authorized and separately budgeted unless and until ALLIS has a market-ready offering, defined pricing, support requirements, customer terms, delivery costs, and an approved contractual pathway.

## Funding Flow

```text
Customer deposits, milestone payments, recurring service revenue,

        ↓
KTS operating account
        ↓
Authorized people and delivery costs
        ↓

marketing, operations, accounting, legal, and customer support
        ↓
```

KTS will maintain separate business records and accounts. KTS funds must remain separate from customer funds, contractor personal funds, Harmony for Hope funds, MountainShares funds, donor funds, grant funds, and other project funds unless a written agreement authorizes a specific payment and accounting arrangement.

## Disbursement Controls

KTS will use the following controls before spending capital:

- Authorize spending through an approved budget, project scope, or operating need.
- Use written contracts, statements of work, invoices, purchase orders, receipts, or equivalent records.
- Require project-specific budgets before authorizing contractor-heavy delivery.
- Use deposits, milestone payments, or confirmed funding authority before beginning costly work.
- Track actual spending against each use-of-funds category monthly.
- Preserve reserves unless there is a documented business need.
- Document material budget changes and revise financial projections when assumptions change.
- Keep personal, nonprofit, donor, grant, customer, contractor, and KTS business funds separate.
- Obtain qualified accounting, tax, payroll, insurance, HR, and legal guidance when needed.

## Funding Priorities

If KTS receives less than the preferred $750,000 target, it will prioritize use of capital in this order:

1. Customer-funded delivery commitments, including approved contractor invoices and direct project costs.
2. Required payroll, founder compensation approved under the operating plan, insurance, tax obligations, and essential operating costs.
3. Software, security, storage, communications, and equipment required to deliver existing customer work.
4. Accounting, legal, HR, contractor administration, and compliance needs.
5. Sales and marketing activities with measurable customer-acquisition value.
6. Equipment upgrades, expanded contractor availability, new capacity, and discretionary growth spending.

KTS will reduce or defer contractor capacity, equipment purchases, marketing spend, and nonessential operating expenses when actual capital, collected revenue, deposits, or contracted backlog do not support the planned level of spending.

## Not a Promise of Expenditure

The allocations in this document are planning ceilings and categories, not guaranteed payments, guaranteed staffing levels, or an obligation to spend every dollar.

KTS may reallocate funds among categories when needed to:

- Meet a lawful customer-delivery obligation.
- Protect payroll, contractor payments, insurance, taxes, and essential operations.
- Respond to actual cost differences or payment delays.
- Address a documented security, equipment, legal, compliance, or delivery need.
- Preserve the organization’s ability to operate responsibly.

Material changes should be documented and reflected in updated financial projections.

## Update Process

KTS will review this document at least quarterly and after any material change in financing, customer contracts, staffing, insurance, equipment, vendor costs, or cash flow.

KTS should compare planned uses with:

- Cash received from financing, deposits, invoices, milestones, and recurring services.
- Actual payroll, employer costs, contractor hours, contractor invoices, and direct project costs.
- Actual marketing spend, lead sources, proposal activity, conversion, paid work, and customer retention.
- Actual software, hosting, insurance, equipment, accounting, legal, HR, and general operating expenses.
- Reserve balances, accounts receivable, accounts payable, customer-payment timing, and project obligations.
- Revenue, gross margin, operating margin, backlog, and available working capital.

## Operating Principle

> KTS uses capital to support real, authorized professional services; pay qualified contributors; protect customer delivery; maintain working capital; and build a disciplined, reliable technical-services organization.

KTS will grow through collected revenue, documented project margins, recurring service agreements, responsible capital use, and transparent financial records.
