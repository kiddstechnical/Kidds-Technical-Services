# Agreement Framework

## Purpose

This document establishes the agreement framework for Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS).

KTS uses written agreements to define what work is authorized, who is responsible, what the customer or partner will pay, how payment occurs, who owns or may use deliverables, how data is handled, how changes are approved, how work is accepted, and how disputes are addressed.

This framework supports KTS work involving mapping, local-information services, websites, digital-preservation work, archives, heritage projects, research, data preparation, training, managed technical support, subcontracting, and related professional services.

This document is an internal operating framework. It is not a contract, legal advice, tax advice, insurance advice, employment advice, or a substitute for review by qualified legal counsel.

KTS will use attorney-reviewed agreement templates appropriate to its jurisdiction, customer type, service line, insurance coverage, privacy obligations, intellectual-property needs, worker-classification requirements, and risk profile.

## Core Rule

> KTS does not begin material work, incur material contractor costs, access restricted customer data, publish customer materials, or commit to ongoing support until the appropriate written authorization and payment pathway exist.

For small, low-risk work, written authorization may be a signed estimate, accepted proposal, customer purchase order, or other attorney-approved acceptance method. For complex, sensitive, recurring, institutional, public, subcontracted, or contractor-heavy work, KTS should use a formal master agreement and statement of work.

## Agreement Architecture

```text
KTS legal and operating policies
        ↓
Master agreement or approved service terms
        ↓
Statement of work, proposal, estimate, or purchase order
        ↓
Data, privacy, security, and access terms
        ↓
Payment schedule, deposit, milestones, invoices, and acceptance
        ↓
Change order or scope amendment when needed
        ↓
Project closeout, handoff, final acceptance, and final invoice
        ↓
Managed-service renewal, support agreement, or follow-on statement of work
```

KTS will use only the documents needed for the specific customer relationship. The complexity of the agreement should match the project’s value, duration, sensitivity, delivery risk, data access, contractor use, public-facing impact, and ongoing-support obligations.

## Agreement Types

| Agreement Type | Primary Use | Typical Parties |
|---|---|---|
| Master Services Agreement (MSA) | Establishes baseline commercial, legal, confidentiality, ownership, limitation, and dispute terms for ongoing work | KTS and customer or partner |
| Statement of Work (SOW) | Defines a specific project, scope, deliverables, timeline, price, payment schedule, acceptance criteria, and exclusions | KTS and customer or partner |
| Proposal or Estimate | Defines smaller or simpler scoped work; may become binding only through approved acceptance language | KTS and customer |
| Managed Services Agreement | Defines recurring support, maintenance, update, hosting coordination, response expectations, renewal, and service limits | KTS and customer |
| Subcontractor Agreement | Defines contractor status, scope, pay, confidentiality, work product, security, invoicing, and compliance obligations | KTS and contractor |
| Partner or Referral Agreement | Defines collaboration, white-label work, referral fees, customer ownership, payment, branding, and non-solicitation rules where lawful | KTS and partner firm |
| Data Processing or Data Access Addendum | Defines permitted access, privacy, security, confidentiality, retention, incident reporting, and return or destruction of data | KTS and customer, partner, or contractor |
| Intellectual Property or Content License | Defines ownership, licenses, permissions, third-party materials, customer-provided content, and reuse rights | KTS and customer, partner, or creator |
| Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) | Protects confidential information before or outside a broader agreement | KTS and prospect, customer, partner, or contractor |
| Change Order | Documents changes to scope, price, schedule, deliverables, responsibilities, or assumptions | KTS and customer or partner |
| Acceptance Certificate or Project Closeout Record | Documents delivery, acceptance, known exceptions, handoff, and final-payment status | KTS and customer |
| Training Agreement or Training Addendum | Defines training format, materials, attendance, fees, travel, recording permissions, and participant responsibilities | KTS and customer |
| Grant-Funded or Public-Entity Addendum | Addresses procurement, funding restrictions, public records, reporting, accessibility, insurance, data, and payment requirements | KTS and public, nonprofit, or grant-funded customer |

KTS may combine documents when attorney-approved templates allow it. A customer’s purchase order or procurement terms may require additional review before KTS accepts them.

## Required Project Documents

| Project Condition | Minimum Documentation |
|---|---|
| Small, low-risk fixed-price work | Written scope, price, payment terms, customer acceptance, and invoice |
| Work over $1,000 | Written proposal or SOW, deposit or payment terms, acceptance criteria, and change-control terms |
| Work over $5,000 | Signed SOW or equivalent, defined milestones, deposit, payment schedule, acceptance criteria, and project contacts |
| Work over $25,000 | Master agreement or formal service agreement, signed SOW, deposit, milestone billing, change order, acceptance process, and project-risk review |
| Recurring support | Managed Services Agreement, scope of included support, service limits, payment in advance, renewal and termination terms |
| Contractor-heavy project | Customer agreement, project budget, contractor agreement, not-to-exceed authorization, confidentiality, data-access terms, and payment protection |
| Data-sensitive or confidential work | Confidentiality terms, data-access rules, security requirements, privacy review, retention rules, and incident-response obligations |
| Archive, heritage, genealogy, cemetery, or sensitive-record work | Ownership and permission confirmation, sensitivity review, publication approval, privacy terms, rights clearance, and access controls |
| Public, nonprofit, grant-funded, or sponsor-funded work | Written funding authority, procurement review, project agreement, reporting requirements, public-records review, and payment pathway |
| Partner or white-label work | Partner agreement or SOW, branding and customer-relationship terms, scope, payment, confidentiality, intellectual-property terms, and acceptance process |

## Minimum Agreement Terms

Each appropriate agreement should address the following matters.

| Agreement Topic | Required Decision |
|---|---|
| Parties | Identify KTS’s correct legal name and the legal entity or authorized customer/partner/contractor name |
| Authority | Confirm that each signer has authority to enter the agreement |
| Scope | Define what KTS will and will not do |
| Deliverables | Define concrete outputs, formats, quantity, quality expectations, and handoff method |
| Timeline | Define start conditions, milestones, customer dependencies, delivery dates, and schedule assumptions |
| Customer responsibilities | Identify materials, data, approvals, access, contacts, feedback, and decisions the customer must provide |
| KTS responsibilities | Identify KTS’s service obligations, project contact, reporting, and delivery process |
| Price | Define fixed price, hourly rate, retainer, subscription, pass-through expense, or other pricing basis |
| Payment | Define deposit, invoices, milestones, payment due dates, late-payment terms, taxes, and expense reimbursement |
| Acceptance | Define how the customer reviews work, the review period, acceptance criteria, revision limits, and deemed acceptance rules if appropriate |
| Change control | Define how scope, price, schedule, deliverables, and assumptions may change |
| Ownership | Define ownership of customer materials, KTS pre-existing materials, final deliverables, source files, data, documentation, and licenses |
| Confidentiality | Define confidential information and permitted use, disclosure, security, and return or destruction obligations |
| Data and privacy | Define permitted data access, data categories, security controls, retention, incident reporting, and deletion or return requirements |
| Third-party materials | Define responsibility for maps, imagery, software, data, fonts, media, open-source components, licenses, permissions, and fees |
| Warranties and disclaimers | Define appropriate service standards and avoid unsupported guarantees |
| Limitation of liability | Allocate risk with attorney-approved limits and exclusions appropriate to the project |
| Indemnity | Define indemnification obligations only with attorney review and appropriate insurance alignment |
| Insurance | Define required coverage, certificates, additional-insured terms, and responsibility for project-specific coverage |
| Term and termination | Define start date, term, renewal, suspension, termination rights, payment obligations, and handoff requirements |
| Dispute process | Define escalation, notice, negotiation, mediation, arbitration, venue, or other attorney-approved process |
| Governing law | Identify the applicable governing law and venue only through attorney-approved terms |
| Publicity | Define whether KTS may identify the customer, display the project, use case-study materials, or issue public statements |
| Accessibility | Define accessibility standards, testing responsibility, content responsibility, and any limitations |
| Records and audit | Define recordkeeping, reporting, audit, grant, procurement, or public-entity obligations when applicable |

## Statement of Work Requirements

Every SOW, proposal, estimate, or work order should answer the following questions.

| Required Section | What It Must Clarify |
|---|---|
| Project name and reference | A unique project title, customer name, agreement reference, and version date |
| Business objective | The customer outcome KTS is engaged to support |
| Scope of services | Specific work KTS will perform |
| Deliverables | What the customer receives and in what format |
| Exclusions | What KTS is not providing |
| Assumptions | Data quality, customer access, approvals, travel, timing, third-party tools, and other conditions |
| Customer responsibilities | Materials, permissions, contacts, decisions, review, hosting, publication, and payment obligations |
| KTS responsibilities | Delivery activities, reporting, meetings, project coordination, and handoff |
| Schedule | Start conditions, milestones, review periods, target dates, and dependency risks |
| Price and expenses | Fees, hourly rates if applicable, pass-through costs, travel, tax, and payment terms |
| Change control | The approval process for additional work, new deliverables, revisions, or changed assumptions |
| Acceptance | Review process, revision limits, acceptance criteria, and final handoff |
| Data and rights | Data access, privacy, permissions, ownership, licensing, and retention |
| Support | Whether any post-delivery support is included and how additional support is priced |
| Project contacts | Authorized decision-makers and day-to-day contacts |
| Signatures or approved acceptance | The method by which the scope becomes authorized |

## Payment and Cash-Flow Rules

KTS uses payment terms that protect customer delivery, contractor payment, operating capacity, and working capital.

| Project Size or Service Type | Planning Payment Structure |
|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | Full payment before delivery or at customer approval |
| $1,000–$5,000 | 50% deposit before work; 50% before launch or handoff |
| $5,000–$25,000 | 40% deposit; 40% at implementation milestone; 20% at customer acceptance |
| Over $25,000 | 25%–40% deposit; monthly or milestone billing; final balance at acceptance |
| Managed service | Monthly, quarterly, or annual payment in advance |
| Training | Full payment in advance, deposit and balance, or approved purchase order |
| Grant-funded, public, nonprofit, sponsor-funded, or partner-funded work | Written funding authority, contract, purchase order, or authorized agreement before contractor-heavy work begins |

KTS will not begin contractor-heavy, travel-heavy, equipment-heavy, vendor-heavy, or data-sensitive work until the required deposit, milestone authorization, purchase order, funding authority, or other documented payment pathway exists.

KTS will not treat verbal assurances, informal interest, prospective grants, expected sponsorships, or unconfirmed funding as authorization to incur material delivery costs.

## Change Control

```text
Customer request, new discovery, changed data, or changed requirement
        ↓
KTS documents the requested change
        ↓
KTS evaluates effect on scope, cost, schedule, contractor capacity,
data handling, ownership, risk, and acceptance criteria
        ↓
KTS issues a written change order, revised SOW, or approved estimate
        ↓
Authorized customer representative approves the change
        ↓
KTS updates budget, schedule, contractor authorizations, and project records
        ↓
KTS begins the changed work
```

KTS will not perform material out-of-scope work merely because the customer requests it informally. Emergency work should be documented as soon as practical and confirmed through an attorney-approved or management-approved process.

## Contractor Agreements

KTS contractor agreements should be reviewed for worker classification, jurisdictional requirements, tax reporting, insurance, and the actual working relationship.

| Contractor Agreement Topic | Required Decision |
|---|---|
| Independent status | Define the intended relationship without misclassifying workers |
| Scope and deliverables | Define authorized work, quality standards, deadlines, and acceptance |
| Rates and payment | Define rates, invoicing, payment timing, expenses, and not-to-exceed limits |
| Authorization | Require a written KTS authorization before work begins or expands |
| Confidentiality | Protect customer, KTS, partner, pricing, technical, and business information |
| Intellectual property | Define ownership or assignment of commissioned work and pre-existing contractor materials |
| Data security | Define access controls, approved systems, credentials, device rules, incident reporting, and data return or deletion |
| Non-solicitation | Include only attorney-approved and lawful restrictions |
| Insurance | Define any contractor insurance requirements appropriate to the work |
| Compliance | Require lawful performance, tax compliance, accessibility, licensing, and third-party-rights compliance |
| Subcontracting | Prohibit or control further subcontracting without KTS’s written approval |
| Termination | Define suspension, termination, handoff, unfinished work, and payment for approved completed work |
| Records | Require accurate time, expense, deliverable, and invoice records |

KTS will use written project budgets, contractor authorizations, time records, invoices, quality review, and not-to-exceed caps. KTS will not authorize contractor work beyond the funded customer scope, approved operating budget, or available working capital.

## Partner and Subcontracting Agreements

KTS may work with web firms, marketing firms, design firms, technology firms, consultants, public organizations, and other partners.

Partner agreements should clarify:

- Which party contracts directly with the end customer.
- Which party has authority to make commitments, approve scope, and accept deliverables.
- Whether KTS is visible to the end customer or provides white-label services.
- How fees, referrals, markups, taxes, expenses, invoices, collections, and nonpayment risk are handled.
- Which party owns customer relationships and communicates with the end customer.
- Who is responsible for project management, approvals, revisions, support, hosting, and warranty obligations.
- How confidential information, customer data, intellectual property, portfolio use, publicity, and branding are handled.
- Whether and how either party may use subcontractors.
- How disputes, customer complaints, scope changes, cancellation, and termination are handled.

KTS will not accept partner terms that create unlimited obligations, unpriced revisions, unclear payment responsibility, unapproved liability exposure, or contractor commitments that are not protected by customer funding and written authorization.

## Data, Privacy, and Security

KTS may handle customer data, community information, business information, public records, archival materials, images, location data, family-history data, contact information, and other potentially sensitive materials.

Before accessing or handling such data, KTS should determine:

- Who owns or controls the data.
- Whether KTS has written permission to access, copy, process, publish, host, map, retain, or share it.
- Whether the data includes personal information, confidential information, protected records, sensitive heritage information, security-sensitive location information, or data subject to legal restrictions.
- Where the data will be stored, who may access it, how it is backed up, and how long it will be retained.
- What security controls, access logs, passwords, encryption, device restrictions, or incident-response procedures are required.
- Whether contractors or third-party vendors need access and whether the customer authorizes that access.
- Whether publication, mapping, digitization, or public access could create safety, privacy, cultural, ethical, legal, or reputational risks.
- Whether the customer must review and approve public-facing materials before release.

KTS will use only approved systems and access methods for customer data. KTS will not upload, publish, disclose, license, sell, or reuse customer data beyond the written authorization and applicable law.

## Intellectual Property and Content

KTS agreements should distinguish among:

| Content or Material | Default Agreement Question |
|---|---|
| Customer-provided materials | Does the customer own or control it, and does KTS have permission to use it for the project? |
| KTS pre-existing tools and methods | KTS generally retains ownership while granting the customer an appropriate use license |
| Final customer-specific deliverables | Does ownership transfer after payment, or does KTS grant a license? |
| Source files, data, templates, scripts, and documentation | Are they included, licensed, withheld, or separately priced? |
| Third-party materials | Who obtains licenses, pays fees, follows attribution rules, and accepts restrictions? |
| Open-source components | What licenses apply, and are customer obligations disclosed? |
| Maps, imagery, data, photographs, scans, and media | Who has permission to use, publish, modify, and retain the material? |
| Portfolio and case-study materials | May KTS identify the customer or display nonconfidential portions after approval? |

KTS will not promise ownership, exclusive rights, confidentiality, unrestricted reuse, or third-party permissions that it does not have authority to provide.

## Acceptance and Closeout

Every material project should have a documented acceptance and closeout process.

| Closeout Step | KTS Action |
|---|---|
| Deliverables provided | Deliver the agreed work through the approved handoff method |
| Customer review | Allow the agreed review period and collect consolidated feedback |
| Revisions | Complete only included revisions or issue a change order for additional work |
| Acceptance | Obtain written acceptance, deemed acceptance if contractually appropriate, or other documented confirmation |
| Final invoice | Issue the final invoice or milestone invoice under the agreement |
| Payment collection | Track payment due date and collection status |
| Handoff | Provide agreed files, credentials, documentation, training, or transfer materials |
| Data disposition | Return, retain, archive, or destroy data according to the agreement and applicable policy |
| Support transition | Start managed service, schedule follow-up, or document post-project support options |
| Portfolio review | Seek authorization before using customer name, logo, materials, outcomes, or testimonials |

## Approval Authority

KTS should maintain an internal approval record for material agreements.

| Agreement or Decision | Recommended KTS Approval |
|---|---|
| Standard small fixed-price work | Authorized KTS business representative |
| Project with contractor cost | Authorized KTS business representative plus documented project budget |
| Project over a KTS-defined threshold | Executive approval and, where appropriate, accounting or legal review |
| Multi-year agreement | Executive approval and legal review |
| Public entity, grant-funded, or procurement contract | Executive approval, legal review, and financial review |
| Data-sensitive, privacy-sensitive, or security-sensitive work | Executive approval plus legal, privacy, security, or insurance review as appropriate |
| Nonstandard intellectual-property, indemnity, insurance, liability, or venue term | Legal review before acceptance |
| Contractor agreement | Executive approval plus classification, tax, insurance, and legal review as appropriate |
| Partner, referral, white-label, or revenue-sharing agreement | Executive approval and legal review |
| Material change order | Project authority approval, customer approval, and budget update |

KTS will define internal dollar thresholds, signing authority, and approval workflows in a separate internal delegation-of-authority policy.

## Agreement Records

KTS should maintain a complete project agreement record containing:

- Executed agreement, MSA, SOW, proposal, estimate, purchase order, or approved acceptance record.
- Customer contact and signer authority information.
- Project budget and pricing calculation.
- Deposit, invoice, payment, accounts-receivable, and collection records.
- Change orders, revised scope, customer approvals, and project communications.
- Contractor agreements, contractor work authorizations, invoices, time records, and deliverable records.
- Data-access approvals, confidentiality records, licenses, permissions, and privacy/security documents.
- Delivery, acceptance, closeout, handoff, training, support, and renewal records.
- Insurance certificates, procurement documents, grant requirements, or public-entity compliance documents when applicable.

KTS will retain records according to its attorney-approved records-retention policy, tax requirements, insurance requirements, customer contracts, and applicable law.

## Red Flags

KTS should pause and obtain qualified review before accepting an agreement that includes:

- Personal guarantees, unlimited liability, or liability terms beyond KTS’s insurance and financial capacity.
- Broad indemnity obligations, particularly for third-party claims, data incidents, intellectual property, or customer-controlled content.
- Unclear scope, undefined deliverables, unlimited revisions, or customer requirements that cannot be priced.
- Payment terms that require KTS to fund material work for an extended period without deposits, milestones, purchase orders, or funding protection.
- Intellectual-property assignment that transfers KTS pre-existing tools, methods, templates, or third-party materials without appropriate pricing or authority.
- Confidentiality, privacy, security, public-records, accessibility, archival, health, financial, educational, or other regulated-data obligations that KTS has not reviewed.
- Exclusivity, non-compete, non-solicitation, revenue-sharing, referral-fee, or restraint provisions.
- International, multi-jurisdictional, government, public-entity, grant-funded, or procurement obligations.
- Requirements to access sensitive locations, handle sensitive community or cultural information, or publish data without verified permission.
- Customer terms incorporated by hyperlink, purchase order, procurement portal, or policy document that KTS has not reviewed.
- Any condition that conflicts with KTS’s ability to pay contributors, protect working capital, or deliver services responsibly.

## Update Process

KTS will review this framework at least annually and whenever there is a material change in:

- Service lines, customer types, pricing, contractor use, managed-service commitments, or delivery methods.
- Applicable laws, insurance coverage, tax treatment, worker-classification guidance, or privacy and security practices.
- KTS operating structure, legal entity, authorized signers, financial controls, or records-retention practices.
- Customer, partner, contractor, insurer, accountant, payroll provider, or legal-counsel requirements.
- Public, grant-funded, archival, heritage, data-sensitive, or high-risk project practices.

## Operating Principle

> Clear written agreements protect customers, contributors, partners, KTS, project funds, data, and delivery quality.

KTS will use agreements to make work understandable, authorized, fairly priced, financially protected, responsibly delivered, and properly documented.
