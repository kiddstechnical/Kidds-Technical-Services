# Small Project Proposal Checklist

> **Kidd’s Technical Services, LLC**  
> Built on trust. Grounded in what’s real.

Use this checklist before sending any KTS proposal, estimate, statement of work, or customer-facing project agreement for a small project.

This checklist supports clear expectations. It does not replace legal advice, insurance requirements, tax obligations, or a signed agreement appropriate to the work.

## Project Identification

- [ ] Customer’s legal or operating name is correct
- [ ] Customer contact name, email, phone number, and billing information are correct
- [ ] Project title clearly describes the work
- [ ] Proposal or estimate number is included
- [ ] Proposal issue date is included
- [ ] Proposal expiration date is included
- [ ] KTS contact information is included
- [ ] Project location, service area, website, platform, venue, or relevant property is identified
- [ ] The customer’s stated problem or intended outcome is written in plain language

## Scope of Work

- [ ] The proposal explains what KTS will do
- [ ] Each deliverable is identified clearly
- [ ] The number of pages, maps, listings, locations, platforms, QR codes, revisions, meetings, or other measurable work items is stated
- [ ] The proposal identifies the information, materials, account access, approvals, or permissions KTS needs from the customer
- [ ] The proposal states whether KTS will publish information, prepare materials for customer publication, or provide handoff materials only
- [ ] The proposal identifies any customer-controlled third-party platforms involved
- [ ] The proposal states the expected start date, delivery timeline, milestone dates, or dependency conditions
- [ ] The proposal identifies what counts as project completion

## Deliverables and Customer Outcome

- [ ] The customer can understand what they will receive
- [ ] Deliverables are described in plain language
- [ ] The expected customer outcome is stated without promising results outside KTS control
- [ ] Any files, links, maps, pages, QR codes, instructions, credentials, reports, or handoff materials are listed
- [ ] Any training, launch support, or post-launch support is defined
- [ ] The proposal states who will own or control each delivered item after final payment
- [ ] The proposal states whether continuing maintenance, updates, hosting, support, or care-plan services are included or excluded

## Exclusions and Boundaries

- [ ] The proposal identifies work that is not included
- [ ] The proposal identifies platforms, accounts, hardware, software, travel, printing, hosting, subscriptions, advertising, permits, licenses, or vendor costs that are excluded unless specifically listed
- [ ] The proposal identifies the number of included revisions
- [ ] The proposal states that additional revisions or new requests require written approval
- [ ] The proposal states that KTS does not provide legal, tax, accounting, engineering, surveying, professional licensing, or other regulated professional services unless separately authorized and qualified
- [ ] The proposal states that KTS does not guarantee Google ranking, Google verification, AI-search results, advertising performance, customer traffic, sales, revenue, grants, funding, permits, approvals, or results controlled by third parties
- [ ] The proposal states that the customer remains responsible for the accuracy, legality, permissions, ownership, and approval of customer-provided information and materials
- [ ] The proposal states that KTS may pause or reschedule work when required customer information, access, approval, payment, or permission is delayed

## Price and Payment

- [ ] The total fixed price, hourly rate, milestone price, or other payment method is stated
- [ ] Any taxes, reimbursable expenses, travel costs, third-party charges, hardware, software, printing, hosting, subscriptions, or platform fees are addressed
- [ ] The required deposit amount and due date are stated
- [ ] For most projects over $1,000, the proposal states a 50% deposit before work begins
- [ ] The remaining payment amount and payment trigger are stated
- [ ] The proposal states whether final payment is due before launch, publication, handoff, delivery, or transfer of final files
- [ ] The proposal identifies acceptable payment methods
- [ ] The proposal identifies invoice due dates and any late-payment terms, if used
- [ ] The proposal states that work outside the approved scope is not authorized without written change approval
- [ ] The proposal states whether the deposit is refundable, nonrefundable, or partially refundable under defined conditions
- [ ] The proposal states what happens to project timing if payment is late

## Customer Responsibilities

- [ ] The customer provides accurate and complete information needed for the work
- [ ] The customer confirms it has the right to use and authorize use of all provided text, logos, photos, maps, data, documents, passwords, account access, and other materials
- [ ] The customer provides timely access to required accounts, properties, locations, personnel, and decision-makers
- [ ] The customer reviews drafts, questions, and approval requests within the stated timeframe
- [ ] The customer provides one identified person with authority to approve work, changes, and publication
- [ ] The customer obtains any required owner, venue, property, agency, platform, copyright, trademark, privacy, or other third-party permissions
- [ ] The customer understands that delays in materials, access, review, approvals, or payment may change the project timeline
- [ ] The customer is responsible for maintaining any customer-controlled account, platform, domain, hosting, listing, or service after handoff unless a separate maintenance agreement applies

## Approvals

- [ ] The proposal identifies which information or deliverables require customer approval before publication, launch, or release
- [ ] The proposal identifies the authorized customer approver
- [ ] The proposal states how approval must be provided, such as email, signed acceptance, portal approval, or written confirmation
- [ ] The proposal states the review period for drafts and approval requests
- [ ] The proposal states whether no response after the review period pauses the project or is treated as approval
- [ ] The proposal states that KTS will not publish customer information without the required approval
- [ ] The proposal documents approval of public-facing location, hours, services, contact details, directions, map details, access information, and other customer information within scope

## Acceptance and Handoff

- [ ] The proposal defines the acceptance criteria for the completed work
- [ ] The customer has a defined review period after delivery
- [ ] The proposal states how the customer reports a material issue within scope
- [ ] The proposal identifies which corrections are included before acceptance
- [ ] The proposal states when final payment is due
- [ ] The proposal states when KTS will transfer final files, links, access instructions, or other handoff materials
- [ ] The proposal states whether ownership or license rights transfer only after full payment
- [ ] The proposal identifies any final customer responsibilities after handoff
- [ ] The proposal states whether ongoing updates, maintenance, or support require a separate agreement

## Change Control

- [ ] The proposal states that any request outside the approved scope is a change request
- [ ] The proposal describes how a customer requests a change
- [ ] The proposal states that KTS will assess the change’s impact on price, timeline, deliverables, and dependencies
- [ ] The proposal states that KTS will provide written change terms before beginning additional work
- [ ] The proposal states that both KTS and the authorized customer representative must approve the change in writing
- [ ] The proposal states that verbal requests, text messages, informal conversations, and assumptions do not change the approved scope unless confirmed in writing
- [ ] The proposal states that KTS may pause affected work until the change is approved and any additional deposit or payment is received

## Data, Access, and Security

- [ ] The proposal identifies whether KTS will receive customer account access, data, credentials, location data, customer data, photos, or other sensitive materials
- [ ] The proposal states the minimum access needed for the project
- [ ] The proposal states that credentials and sensitive information must not be placed in public repositories, public documents, or unapproved channels
- [ ] The proposal states how customer access will be removed, returned, or transferred at project completion
- [ ] The proposal identifies any customer data retention, deletion, backup, or storage expectations that apply
- [ ] The proposal identifies any confidentiality expectations or separate confidentiality agreement requirements
- [ ] The proposal identifies any security limitations or third-party platform responsibilities

## Final Review Before Sending

- [ ] The proposal is internally reviewed for clear language, correct names, correct price, correct payment schedule, and correct timeline
- [ ] Every promise is within KTS’s actual authority, capability, insurance, qualifications, and written scope
- [ ] No promise relies on unverified funding, future hiring, unapproved subcontractors, unavailable technology, or third-party approval
- [ ] The proposal does not include passwords, API keys, account credentials, financial-account details, private personal information, or sensitive customer information
- [ ] All referenced attachments, maps, schedules, examples, terms, and supporting documents are included
- [ ] The customer receives a complete copy of the proposal
- [ ] KTS retains a dated copy of the sent proposal
- [ ] The approved and signed version is saved with the project records before work begins

---

## Minimum Proposal Summary

Every KTS small-project proposal should be able to answer these questions clearly:

1. What problem is KTS helping solve?
2. What exactly will KTS deliver?
3. What is not included?
4. What does it cost, and when is payment due?
5. What must the customer provide, approve, or authorize?
6. Who has authority to approve the work?
7. How will the customer know the work is complete?
8. How are additions, changes, delays, or new requests handled?
9. What information, access, or data will KTS receive and how will it be handled?
10. What happens after final payment and handoff?
