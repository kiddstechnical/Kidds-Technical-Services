# Related-Party Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the operational, financial, governance, and public-communication boundaries among Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS), its founder and owner Carrie Kidd, contracted contributors, and any related nonprofit, community, or affiliated organizations.

These boundaries protect customers, partners, funders, contractors, and each organization by making roles, ownership, payment, decision-making, and use of resources clear.

## Kidd's Technical Services, LLC

Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS) is a separate commercial business owned and directed by Carrie Kidd.

KTS provides fee-for-service work, including local-business visibility services, mapping, public-information tools, digital preservation, data preparation, web and kiosk experiences, ALLIS-related services, technical implementation, managed support, training, and related project services.

KTS owns or controls its own business operations, contracts, customer relationships, work product, operating accounts, expenses, pricing, invoices, equipment, intellectual-property rights, and commercial revenue, except where a written agreement states otherwise.

KTS may hire employees, engage independent contractors, purchase services, seek financing, receive customer payments, and enter commercial agreements in its own name.

## Carrie Kidd

Carrie Kidd is the founder and owner of KTS. She may also hold leadership, volunteer, board, contractor, employee, or other roles with separate organizations.

A role held by Carrie with another organization does not make that organization an owner, partner, customer, funder, employee, contractor, agent, or representative of KTS unless a separate written agreement expressly establishes that relationship.

Carrie must keep KTS business decisions, records, invoices, contracts, funds, and property separate from the decisions, records, funds, and property of any other organization with which she is involved.

## Harmony for Hope, Inc.

Harmony for Hope, Inc. is a separate nonprofit organization and is not an owner of KTS or ALLIS.

Harmony for Hope does not receive KTS commercial revenue, own KTS customer relationships, direct KTS operations, or control KTS intellectual property unless a specific written agreement states otherwise.

KTS is not a program, department, subsidiary, trade name, or automatic vendor of Harmony for Hope.

Harmony for Hope may independently choose to:

- Sponsor or support a qualifying community-benefit project consistent with its charitable mission.
- Purchase KTS services through a written agreement and approved nonprofit decision-making process.
- Refer a potential project to KTS or receive a referral from KTS when appropriate.
- Collaborate on a project when each organization has a defined role, documented deliverables, and written terms.

Any transaction between KTS and Harmony for Hope must be documented in writing and must identify the scope, price or payment terms, funding source, deliverables, ownership, public-use rights, data stewardship, approval process, and conflict-of-interest safeguards.

## Contracted Contributors

KTS may engage Aubrey Dickerson and other qualified contributors on a contract basis when their experience matches a project's scope, schedule, budget, and customer requirements.

Contracted contributors are not automatically owners, officers, partners, employees, agents, or authorized signatories of KTS. Their authority is limited to the written scope of their contract or project assignment.

Each contractor agreement should address, as appropriate:

- Scope of work and deliverables.
- Compensation and payment timing.
- Confidentiality and handling of customer information.
- Ownership or license rights for work product.
- Access to customer systems, data, accounts, and physical locations.
- Customer communication and approval boundaries.
- Security, safety, insurance, and legal requirements.
- Independent-contractor status and tax responsibility.

KTS remains responsible for customer communication, scope control, quality assurance, governance, and final delivery unless a written customer agreement states otherwise.

## Customer and Collection Steward Authority

Customers and collection stewards retain authority over their own information, materials, branding, public-facing content, access rules, and approval decisions.

KTS does not claim ownership of customer-provided maps, records, photographs, logos, data, documents, or other materials unless ownership or licensing terms are expressly stated in writing.

Before public release, KTS will obtain the customer's or collection steward's approval for public-facing content, access settings, and use of customer materials within the agreed project scope.

## Funding and Financial Separation

KTS may pursue customer revenue, commercial financing, loans, sponsorships, grants available to eligible businesses, or other lawful funding sources in its own name.

KTS funds must be deposited into and paid from KTS-controlled accounts. KTS expenses, invoices, contracts, tax records, payroll, contractor payments, and financial statements must remain separate from those of Harmony for Hope or any other organization.

A nonprofit grant, charitable contribution, restricted donation, or public-benefit award received by another organization is not KTS revenue unless:

1. The funding source permits the organization to purchase KTS services.
2. The organization follows its applicable approval and conflict-of-interest process.
3. KTS and the organization enter a written agreement.
4. Payment to KTS is for defined, reasonable, documented services or deliverables.

KTS will not represent a donation to a nonprofit as a payment to KTS, nor represent KTS commercial revenue as charitable funds.

## Conflicts of Interest

When KTS is considered for work by an organization with which Carrie Kidd, Aubrey Dickerson, or another KTS contributor has a leadership, board, employee, volunteer, family, financial, or other material relationship, the relationship must be disclosed to the organization before a contract decision is made.

The organization should use its own conflict-of-interest policy and independent approval process. Where appropriate, the interested person should not participate in the final decision to select, negotiate with, or approve KTS.

KTS should use written scopes, reasonable pricing, invoices, deliverable acceptance, and separate records to demonstrate that any transaction is fair, necessary, and properly authorized.

## Intellectual Property and ALLIS

ALLIS is the KTS platform for locally organized knowledge, map-based access, retrieval-grounded information experiences, and locally governed deployment options.

KTS retains ownership of ALLIS and pre-existing KTS methods, templates, software, documentation, branding, and technical materials unless a written agreement provides a specific license, assignment, or ownership arrangement.

Customers retain ownership of their own pre-existing materials and approved collections. Project agreements must state the rights each party receives in the final deliverables, including source files, data exports, public content, software configuration, and ongoing access.

No customer, contractor, nonprofit, sponsor, or collaborator receives ownership of ALLIS merely because it funds, uses, hosts, promotes, tests, or contributes material to a KTS project.

## Public Communications

KTS, Harmony for Hope, contractors, customers, and collaborators should accurately describe their relationship in public materials.

Unless a written agreement provides otherwise:

- KTS should be described as an independent, founder-led commercial technical-services business.
- Harmony for Hope should be described as a separate nonprofit organization.
- Aubrey Dickerson should be described as contracted project support when working under a KTS contract.
- A sponsored or collaborative project should identify each participating organization and its actual role.
- No party should imply ownership, employment, partnership, endorsement, agency, or funding responsibility that has not been documented.

## Recordkeeping and Review

KTS will maintain appropriate records for commercial engagements, including proposals, contracts, scopes of work, approvals, invoices, contractor agreements, payment records, deliverable-acceptance records, and relevant ownership or license terms.

This document is an operational framework, not legal or tax advice. KTS should obtain qualified legal, tax, insurance, accounting, and nonprofit-governance advice before entering transactions involving related organizations, restricted funds, board members, family relationships, intellectual-property licensing, regulated data, or material conflicts of interest.

KTS should review and update these boundaries when its ownership, contractor structure, nonprofit relationships, funding sources, services, or platform licensing changes.
