# Leadership and Experience

## Kidd's Technical Services, LLC

Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS) is founder-led by Carrie Kidd and supported, when appropriate, by contracted project contributors. KTS is intentionally lean: it combines accountable leadership, practical technical work, and specialized support matched to the needs of each engagement.

## Carrie Kidd

**Founder and Owner**  
Mount Hope, West Virginia

Carrie Kidd leads KTS client relationships, project direction, platform development, information governance, and service delivery. She founded KTS to help businesses, organizations, and communities make local information easier to find, understand, preserve, and use.

Her work brings together place-based knowledge, mapping and information design, digital preservation, public access, and locally governed technology. Through KTS and the Artificial Learning & Location Intelligence System (ALLIS), she develops practical tools for local-business visibility, public information, heritage and trail experiences, map collections, and organization-specific knowledge access.

Carrie is responsible for the scope, stewardship approach, client approvals, and delivery standards for KTS engagements.

## Delivery Model

KTS remains accountable to each customer through a clear founder-led delivery model:

- Carrie Kidd directs each engagement and remains responsible for client communication, scope, approvals, governance, and final delivery.
- Contracted contributors are engaged only when their experience fits the project scope and customer needs.
- KTS documents roles, deliverables, access, and stewardship responsibilities for each project.
- Collection stewards and customers retain authority over their own approved materials, information, and public-facing content.

This model allows KTS to provide personal, local service while adding the right expertise for mapping, information access, community-facing tools, and technical implementation.
