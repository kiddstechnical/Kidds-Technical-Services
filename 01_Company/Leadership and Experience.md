# Leadership and Experience

## Kidd's Technical Services, LLC

Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS) is a founder-led technical-services business directed by Carrie Kidd. KTS is intentionally lean and designed to grow through paid customer work, accountable project management, and qualified contracted support.

KTS does not depend on one person performing every technical, mapping, web, field, documentation, or customer-support task. Instead, KTS defines the customer need, manages the work, maintains delivery standards, and engages appropriate contributors when specialized capacity is needed.

## Delivery Model

KTS remains accountable to every customer through a clear founder-led delivery model:

- Carrie Kidd directs each engagement and remains responsible for client communication, scope, approvals, governance, quality control, and final delivery.
- KTS engages contracted contributors when their experience fits the project scope, customer needs, schedule, and available project budget.
- KTS documents roles, deliverables, access, confidentiality, and stewardship responsibilities for each project.
- Customers and collection stewards retain authority over their own approved materials, information, and public-facing content.
- KTS may coordinate qualified contractors for mapping, web implementation, field documentation, accessibility, data preparation, digitization, technical setup, and customer-support work.

This approach enables KTS to provide personal, local, accountable service while adding the right expertise and delivery capacity for each project.

## Workforce and Contractor Strategy

KTS is structured to create paid project opportunities for qualified local contractors and contributors rather than requiring the founder to perform every task alone. As customer work is secured, KTS may engage specialized support for mapping, web implementation, field documentation, accessibility, data preparation, technical setup, digitization, public-information tools, and customer support.

This strategy allows KTS to match skills to actual customer needs, expand delivery capacity responsibly, and build practical local technical employment through revenue-producing work. Contractors are engaged under project-specific agreements and are paid from approved project budgets or available operating funds.

KTS may pursue appropriate working-capital financing to support contract labor, project-delivery costs, software and hosting, equipment, insurance, marketing, and other eligible business operating expenses. Any financing use is subject to the terms, requirements, approvals, and permitted-use rules of the relevant lender or funding program.
