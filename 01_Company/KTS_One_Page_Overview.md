# Kidd's Technical Services

## Company overview

**Company:** Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS)  
**Founder and owner:** Carrie Kidd  
**Location:** Oak Hill, West Virginia  
**Primary platform:** Artificial Learning & Location Intelligence System (ALLIS)  
**Business focus:** Practical geospatial, mapping, local-information, and digital-preservation services for organizations and small businesses

---

## What KTS does

Kidd's Technical Services turns local information into useful maps, public tools, and locally managed digital archives.

KTS helps customers get found, explain where they operate, organize their local knowledge, and make approved information easier for customers, residents, visitors, or staff to use. We create grant-ready maps, service-area maps, public web maps, trail and heritage maps, digital map collections, QR-linked information pages, and locally governed ALLIS-node experiences.

For collections and public-information projects, KTS digitizes and organizes approved maps and records, prepares them for preservation and access, and delivers them through web, kiosk, local Wi-Fi, staff-only, or offline-capable interfaces. Collection stewards retain ownership and authority over their materials. KTS provides the technical implementation, managed operation, maintenance, support, and documented export package.

## Who KTS serves

KTS works with people and organizations that need local information to be easier to find, understand, manage, or share:

- Restaurants, retailers, salons, contractors, home-service businesses, venues, campgrounds, makers, and other small businesses.
- Municipalities, counties, and regional-development organizations.
- Libraries, archives, museums, and historical societies.
- Universities, schools, and research partners.
- Tourism, heritage, trail, downtown, and Main Street organizations.
- Nonprofits, community organizations, and grant applicants.
- Property owners, developers, and service providers that need clear site, access, or service-area visualizations.

Highly regulated uses, including healthcare, public safety, emergency response, and utility operations, may be considered only after KTS and the customer complete the required legal, security, data-governance, and integration review.

## How a project works

1. KTS starts with the customer's immediate need: getting found, explaining a location, mapping a service area, preparing a grant, organizing a collection, or creating public access to approved information.
2. KTS defines the scope, intended users, source materials, rights, access levels, delivery format, schedule, price, and success criteria.
3. KTS prepares the map, data, public page, QR content, web experience, or approved ALLIS-node configuration.
4. The customer or collection steward reviews and approves public-facing material before launch.
5. KTS delivers the agreed materials and, when included, provides training, maintenance, support, updates, and documented export services.

## Funding and engagement

KTS works through fee-for-service agreements for discovery, mapping, digitization, deployment, managed operation, training, and support. Small-business projects may be customer-funded. Public-benefit and collection projects may use municipal, county, state, federal, academic, nonprofit, philanthropic, private-sponsorship, tourism, regional-development, or blended funding pathways.

Harmony for Hope, Inc. may sponsor a qualifying community-benefit deployment when the project fits its charitable mission. Harmony for Hope is not required for KTS to provide services, does not own ALLIS, and does not receive KTS commercial revenue unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

## Why KTS

KTS combines geospatial systems work with practical, place-based service. A small business may need customers to find it on a map. A nonprofit may need a clear map for a grant application. A heritage group may need an archive or walking trail brought to life. A town may need public information organized into one usable map.

KTS starts with the practical result the customer needs today, then scales into managed mapping, digital preservation, and ALLIS-node services when the project requires them. ALLIS is the KTS platform for locally organized knowledge, map-based access, retrieval-grounded information experiences, and locally governed deployment options.
