# Kidd's Technical Services

> **Built on trust. Grounded in what’s real.**

In an age when it can be difficult to tell what is real, edited, or artificially generated, Kidd's Technical Services helps organizations present approved information with the authenticity, context, care, and accountability it deserves.

**Company:** Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS)

**Business focus:** Practical geospatial, mapping, local-information, digital-preservation, training, and managed-support services for organizations and small businesses

## What KTS does

Kidd's Technical Services turns approved local information into useful maps, public tools, and locally managed digital archives.

KTS helps customers get found, explain where they operate, organize their local knowledge, and make approved information easier for customers, residents, visitors, or staff to use. KTS creates grant-ready maps, service-area maps, public web maps, trail and heritage maps, digital map collections, QR-linked information pages, and practical web-connected information tools.

For collections and public-information projects, KTS digitizes and organizes approved maps and records, prepares them for preservation and access, and delivers them through web, kiosk, local Wi-Fi, staff-only, or offline-capable interfaces. Collection stewards retain ownership and authority over their materials. KTS provides technical implementation, managed operation, maintenance, support, and documented export packages.

KTS works through written scopes, approved data access, defined deliverables, documented payment terms, customer review, and appropriate acceptance processes.

## How a project works

```text
Customer need or information challenge
        ↓
Discovery, scope, pricing, and written authorization
        ↓
Approved data, materials, permissions, and customer input
        ↓
Mapping, preservation, web-connected delivery, training,
technical production, or managed support
        ↓
Customer review, acceptance, handoff, and final invoicing
        ↓
Optional updates, support, training, or follow-on work
```

1. KTS starts with the customer's immediate need: getting found, explaining a location, mapping a service area, preparing a grant, organizing a collection, or creating public access to approved information.
2. KTS defines the scope, intended users, source materials, rights, access levels, delivery format, schedule, price, payment terms, and success criteria.
3. KTS prepares the map, data, public page, QR content, web experience, archive-access tool, training material, or other approved deliverable.
4. The customer or collection steward reviews and approves public-facing material before launch.
5. KTS delivers the agreed materials and, when included, provides training, maintenance, support, updates, and documented export services.

KTS does not treat a lead, informal discussion, grant application, verbal interest, or potential partnership as authorized work or revenue. Material work begins only after an appropriate agreement, funding or payment pathway, and project authorization are in place.

## Trust and stewardship

KTS earns trust through clear scopes, approved data access, documented permissions, accurate claims, transparent pricing, customer review, and accountable delivery.

- **Collection stewards retain control.** Customers and lawful collection stewards retain ownership and decision-making authority over their records, materials, and approved uses.
- **Work must be authorized.** KTS uses written scopes, agreements, payment terms, permissions, and acceptance criteria appropriate to the work.
- **Data requires care.** KTS evaluates privacy, security, licensing, sensitivity, access, retention, and publication before handling or releasing information.
- **Authenticity deserves context.** KTS helps customers present approved information with appropriate attribution, source awareness, human context, and stewardship.
- **Marketing claims must be accurate.** KTS does not present planning assumptions, future concepts, unverified outcomes, or unavailable offerings as completed services.
- **Customer funds and KTS funds remain separate.** KTS maintains separate business records and does not mix customer, donor, nonprofit, contractor, grant, or personal funds without written authorization.

## Financial discipline

KTS works through fee-for-service agreements for discovery, mapping, digitization, technical delivery, managed operation, training, and support.

Small-business projects may be customer-funded. Public-benefit and collection projects may use municipal, county, state, federal, academic, nonprofit, philanthropic, private-sponsorship, tourism, regional-development, or blended funding pathways.

```text
Customer deposit, milestone payment, recurring service revenue,
authorized funding, or lawful business capital
        ↓
KTS operating account
        ↓
Authorized delivery, payroll, contractor invoices,
software, insurance, equipment, marketing, and operations
        ↓
Customer delivery, acceptance, invoicing, collections,
reserve, reinvestment, and sustainable capacity
```

KTS plans services and staffing around actual customer work, documented direct costs, gross-margin discipline, deposits, milestone billing, recurring service agreements, and working capital. KTS does not create unfunded payroll, contractor, vendor, or customer-delivery obligations based solely on projected revenue.

Harmony for Hope, Inc. may sponsor a qualifying community-benefit project when it fits that organization’s charitable mission. Harmony for Hope is not required for KTS to provide services and does not receive KTS commercial revenue unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.
