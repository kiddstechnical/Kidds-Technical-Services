# Kidd's Technical Services

## Company overview

**Company:** Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS)  
**Founder and owner:** Carrie Kidd  
**Location:** Oak Hill, West Virginia  
**Primary platform:** Artificial Learning & Location Intelligence System (ALLIS)  
**Business focus:** Locally governed digital infrastructure for maps, heritage records, public information, and organization-specific knowledge

---

## What KTS does

Kidd's Technical Services helps organizations preserve, organize, and share approved place-based information.

KTS digitizes map collections and local records, prepares them for preservation and public use, and delivers them through locally governed ALLIS nodes. An ALLIS node can provide web, kiosk, local Wi-Fi, staff-only, or offline-capable access to approved maps, heritage materials, public information, and organization-specific knowledge.

Collection stewards retain ownership and authority over their materials. KTS provides the technical implementation, managed operation, maintenance, support, and exportable preservation package.

## Initial services

| Service | Customer outcome |
|---|---|
| Digital heritage map preservation | Approved maps and place-based records are digitized, organized, documented, and prepared for long-term access |
| ALLIS node deployment | A locally governed node serves approved map collections, public information, or organization-specific knowledge through an agreed access experience |
| Public map and information experience | Residents, visitors, researchers, or staff use a web map, kiosk, local Wi-Fi landing page, or offline-capable interface |
| Managed preservation and access | KTS provides backups, integrity checks, software updates, technical support, and documented data export under a service agreement |

## Who KTS serves

KTS initially serves organizations that hold, create, or need to share place-based information:

- Municipalities, counties, and regional-development organizations.
- Libraries, archives, museums, and historical societies.
- Universities, schools, and research partners.
- Tourism, heritage, trail, downtown, and Main Street organizations.
- Nonprofits and community organizations.
- Small and midsize businesses that need location-aware document retrieval, map-based public information, or locally governed knowledge tools.

Highly regulated uses, including healthcare, public safety, emergency response, and utility operations, may be considered only after KTS and the customer complete the required legal, security, data-governance, and integration review.

## How a project works

1. KTS and the customer define the collection, use case, users, rights, access levels, site requirements, and success criteria.
2. KTS inventories, digitizes, ingests, organizes, and documents approved materials.
3. KTS configures an ALLIS node or approved hosting environment for the agreed access model.
4. The collection steward reviews and approves public-facing content before launch.
5. KTS deploys the approved experience and provides training, support, maintenance, and export services under the agreement.

## Funding and engagement

KTS works through fee-for-service agreements for discovery, digitization, deployment, managed operation, training, and support. A project may be funded through municipal, county, state, federal, academic, nonprofit, philanthropic, private-sponsorship, tourism, regional-development, or blended pathways.

Harmony for Hope, Inc. may sponsor a qualifying community-benefit deployment when the project fits its charitable mission. Harmony for Hope is not required for KTS to provide services, does not own ALLIS, and does not receive KTS commercial revenue unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

## Why KTS

KTS combines technical systems work with place-based community knowledge. Rather than requiring every collection to live only in a distant cloud service, KTS helps partners keep approved materials under documented local governance while making them useful through contemporary web, kiosk, local-network, and offline-capable tools.

ALLIS is the KTS platform for delivering these services. The platform supports locally organized knowledge, map-based access, retrieval-grounded information experiences, and deployment models suited to rural, heritage, and community settings.
