# Bootstrap Operating Stack

> **Kidd’s Technical Services, LLC**  
> Built on trust. Grounded in what’s real.

This document records the minimum operating stack KTS can use to begin serving local-business customers without assuming unnecessary spending, payroll, subscriptions, storage, equipment, or external commitments.

KTS will use tools already available where appropriate, purchase only what is required to deliver approved customer work, and document actual costs before relying on any planning estimate.

This is an internal operating document. It is not a commitment to purchase, subscribe, hire, host customer data, provide a particular service, or maintain any specific tool indefinitely.

## Operating Principle

> Use existing capability first. Add cost only when a real customer need, signed scope, collected deposit, documented operational requirement, or approved working-capital plan supports it.

KTS will keep company finances, customer funds, contractor funds, personal funds, Harmony for Hope funds, MountainShares funds, donor funds, grant funds, and other entity funds separate unless a written agreement expressly authorizes a defined transaction.

## Website and Public Information

The KTS website is the primary public source for KTS’s own services, contact information, service boundaries, offers, project examples, and customer-facing materials.

Before KTS directs customers to the website, confirm that it includes:

- KTS’s correct business name and contact information
- A clear description of the current service offerings
- The “Trusted Local Information” positioning
- A clear statement that KTS does not promise third-party rankings, verification, traffic, sales, advertising outcomes, or AI-search results
- A method for customers to request a conversation or proposal
- Any required privacy, accessibility, terms, or legal notices appropriate to the site and services
- No passwords, API keys, private customer data, private addresses, or other sensitive information
- Current links, forms, QR codes, and contact methods

## Business Email

KTS should use an email address that customers can recognize, that KTS can access reliably, and that has clear ownership and continuity.

If KTS uses existing SiteGround-hosted email capacity, document:

- The domain and mailbox being used
- Who owns the SiteGround account
- Who can access the mailbox and administrative settings
- How KTS will retain access if a personal or unrelated account changes
- Whether messages and attachments are backed up
- Whether the arrangement is appropriate for customer records and confidential information
- The process for removing access when a person or contractor no longer needs it

KTS should transition to an independently controlled KTS business email arrangement when continued operation, privacy, branding, customer trust, or business continuity requires it.

## QGIS and Mapping Work

QGIS is the default starter tool for KTS geospatial and mapping work because it can support map preparation and data review without an initial license cost.

Before delivering a map or location-related product, KTS will confirm:

- The map purpose and intended audience
- The source and permitted use of each dataset, basemap, photo, logo, and customer-provided material
- The appropriate level of precision for the customer’s use case
- The customer’s approval of public-facing locations, entrances, parking, access routes, service areas, and contact information
- Whether the map may create safety, privacy, access, property-rights, or accessibility concerns
- Whether the work requires a licensed surveyor, engineer, planner, emergency-management professional, or other qualified professional
- That the final deliverable includes appropriate attribution, disclaimers, dates, and source notes where needed

KTS will not represent general-purpose customer maps as surveys, legal descriptions, engineering plans, emergency directions, or authoritative government records unless properly qualified, authorized, and documented.

## Bookkeeping and Financial Records

KTS will establish a reliable bookkeeping process before or when it begins invoicing, receiving customer deposits, paying contractors, or making business purchases.

At a minimum, KTS will record:

- Customer name and project reference
- Proposal amount, invoice number, invoice date, due date, deposit amount, and final payment amount
- Payment date, payment method, and any refund or credit
- Direct project costs, including contractor, travel, printing, software, platform, material, and vendor costs
- Business operating costs, including domain, email, storage, insurance, equipment, accounting, and professional support
- Receipts, invoices, contracts, proposals, change approvals, customer approvals, and project handoff records
- Tax-related records and the business purpose of each expense
- Any owner contribution, reimbursement, loan, grant, or other funding transaction, with its source and authorization

KTS will review cash on hand, unpaid invoices, deposits received, direct project costs, recurring expenses, and upcoming obligations at least monthly.

## Storage, Backup, and Data Handling

KTS will use an organized project-folder structure and maintain access only for people who need it to perform approved work.

A project folder should include, as applicable:

```text
Customer_Name/
├── 01_Proposal_and_Agreement/
├── 02_Customer_Provided_Materials/
├── 03_Working_Files/
├── 04_Approvals_and_Change_Requests/
├── 05_Final_Deliverables/
├── 06_Invoices_and_Payment_Records/
└── 07_Handoff_and_Closeout/
```

KTS will:

- Use clear file names and dates
- Keep a copy of approved proposals, approvals, and final delivered materials
- Maintain at least one backup copy of critical business and project records
- Limit access to customer files and accounts
- Separate public portfolio material from customer working files
- Confirm permission before publishing customer logos, maps, photos, data, locations, testimonials, or case studies
- Follow the customer agreement for retention, return, deletion, and access requirements
- Remove contractor or temporary access when it is no longer needed

KTS will not:

- Commit customer credentials, API keys, passwords, private keys, tokens, database files, payment information, or private personal data to GitHub or another public repository
- Use a personal, donor, nonprofit, or unrelated organization’s storage as KTS customer storage without documented permission and appropriate safeguards
- Promise unlimited storage, perpetual retention, regulated-data compliance, or disaster recovery unless specifically designed, authorized, and contracted

## Equipment Purchases

Before purchasing equipment, KTS will document:

- The customer or operational need
- Why existing equipment cannot meet the need
- The estimated cost, tax, shipping, accessories, setup, maintenance, warranty, and replacement implications
- Whether the purchase is a direct customer-project cost, a shared operating asset, or an optional upgrade
- The expected effect on delivery quality, safety, efficiency, or revenue
- The available cash, approved funding source, and payment method
- Whether the customer must approve the cost before KTS incurs it

Examples of equipment that may be considered only when justified include:

- Reliable business laptop or workstation
- External backup drive or secure storage device
- Field tablet or mobile device
- Camera or basic documentation equipment
- Portable printer or display materials
- GPS or field-data collection tools
- Accessibility, safety, or fieldwork equipment

## Insurance and Risk Review

Before beginning a service that involves site visits, customer property, field work, physical maps or directions, public events, contractor work, access to customer accounts, customer data, or other material risk, KTS will review whether insurance or additional contractual protection is required.

KTS will obtain actual quotes and confirm coverage before making any insurance claim in marketing, proposals, or customer conversations.

Potential areas to review may include:

- General liability
- Professional or errors-and-omissions coverage
- Cyber or data-related coverage
- Commercial auto or business-use auto coverage
- Equipment coverage
- Workers’ compensation requirements
- Contractor insurance requirements
- Event, venue, or property-specific insurance requirements

Coverage needs depend on the actual work, location, customer agreement, business structure, and applicable requirements. KTS should obtain qualified insurance and legal guidance when needed.

## Minimum Readiness Check

Before accepting the first paid project, confirm:

- [ ] KTS has a clear customer-facing offer and service boundaries
- [ ] KTS can issue a written proposal, invoice, and receipt
- [ ] KTS has a documented deposit and final-payment process
- [ ] KTS has a functioning business contact method
- [ ] KTS has a defined location for project records, approvals, and final deliverables
- [ ] KTS has a backup process for critical records
- [ ] KTS can receive and track payment separately from personal or other-entity funds
- [ ] KTS has confirmed whether insurance is required for the proposed work
- [ ] KTS has a process for customer approvals and change requests
- [ ] KTS will not use unapproved customer information, credentials, photos, data, or location details
- [ ] KTS has reviewed the project’s direct costs, timeline, and delivery capacity before accepting payment
- [ ] KTS has saved the approved proposal and customer authorization before beginning material work
