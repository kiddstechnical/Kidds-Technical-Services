# ALLIS Node Deployment and Managed Service

## Purpose

Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS) deploys and supports Artificial Learning & Location Intelligence System (ALLIS) nodes for organizations that need practical, locally governed access to approved information.

An ALLIS node is a configured information-access environment that can organize and deliver approved maps, records, documents, public information, organizational knowledge, and place-based materials. Depending on the project, access may be provided through a public web experience, staff-only interface, kiosk, local Wi-Fi landing page, controlled network, or offline-capable deployment.

The purpose is not to replace the customer's authority over its information. The purpose is to make approved information easier to find, understand, preserve, and use.

## What an ALLIS Node Can Do

An ALLIS node may be configured to support one or more of the following uses:

- Public maps, points of interest, directions, visitor information, trails, heritage sites, and local resources.
- Digital access to approved archival maps, photographs, records, reports, and place-based collections.
- Staff-facing retrieval of approved procedures, reference materials, organizational documents, and location-aware information.
- Kiosk or local-network access where internet connectivity is limited, unreliable, unnecessary, or not appropriate for the use case.
- QR-linked information pages for businesses, events, historic sites, trail locations, facilities, services, or public resources.
- Search and retrieval experiences grounded in the customer's approved materials and configured access rules.
- Digital preservation packages, metadata, source-file organization, and documented data export.

Each deployment is scoped to the customer’s actual need. KTS does not represent ALLIS as a substitute for professional legal, medical, engineering, emergency-response, surveying, public-safety, or regulatory systems.

## Access and Governance

ALLIS is designed around local stewardship and documented access boundaries.

The customer or collection steward retains authority over its own approved materials, access rules, branding, public-facing content, and release decisions. KTS configures and operates the technical environment under the terms of the project agreement.

Before launch, the customer reviews and approves:

- The materials included in the node.
- The intended users and access model.
- Public-facing text, maps, images, links, and search behavior.
- Permissions for staff, customers, visitors, researchers, partners, or the public.
- Hosting, backup, update, export, and support expectations.

KTS does not claim ownership of customer-provided collections, maps, records, data, photographs, documents, or branding unless a written agreement states otherwise.

## Deployment Models

KTS may offer an ALLIS node through one or more of the following models.

| Model | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Public web node | Public maps, visitor information, heritage collections, business information, resource directories, and approved public knowledge |
| Staff or member node | Internal procedures, approved reference materials, controlled organizational knowledge, and staff-facing maps |
| Kiosk node | Libraries, museums, visitor centers, storefronts, events, trailheads, and community locations |
| Local Wi-Fi node | On-site access through a customer-controlled Wi-Fi landing page or local network |
| Offline-capable node | Rural, field, archival, event, or continuity settings where dependable internet access cannot be assumed |
| Hybrid node | A combination of public web, staff access, kiosk, local Wi-Fi, and offline-capable use |

The selected model depends on the collection, intended users, connectivity, privacy needs, site conditions, budget, and support requirements.

Unless separately contracted and appropriately qualified, KTS does not provide:

- Land surveying, boundary determination, title research, or legal property opinions.
- Engineering design, construction certification, or safety certification.
- Emergency dispatch, emergency-command systems, or real-time public-safety operations.
- Medical, legal, financial, insurance, or regulatory advice.
- Guaranteed search ranking, uninterrupted third-party service, or permanent compatibility with outside platforms.
- Archival, records-retention, privacy, accessibility, cybersecurity, or regulatory compliance certification.

Projects involving sensitive, regulated, restricted, or safety-critical information require a separate review of legal, security, governance, access, and integration requirements before KTS accepts the work.

## Project Path

1. **Discovery:** KTS and the customer define the information need, intended users, materials, rights, access model, site conditions, budget, and first-phase outcome.
2. **Scope and agreement:** KTS documents deliverables, responsibilities, price, schedule, approvals, ownership, support expectations, and export terms.
3. **Preparation:** KTS prepares approved materials, maps, records, metadata, access controls, and interface content.
4. **Configuration:** KTS configures the agreed ALLIS node and access experience.
5. **Review:** The customer reviews content, access rules, and user experience before launch.
6. **Launch and handoff:** KTS deploys the approved node, trains designated contacts, and provides agreed documentation.
7. **Managed operation or export:** The customer selects ongoing KTS managed service, a defined update arrangement, or documented export and handoff under the agreement.

## Engagement

KTS begins each ALLIS-node conversation with a practical first question:

> What approved information do your users need to find, understand, preserve, or use—and what is the simplest reliable way to give them access?

The answer may lead to a limited pilot, a map or archive project, a public web experience, a kiosk, a local Wi-Fi tool, a staff-facing knowledge environment, an offline-capable node, or a phased managed-service deployment.
