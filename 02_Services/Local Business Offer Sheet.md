> **Kidd’s Technical Services, LLC**

## Why It Matters

Customers should not have to guess whether a business is open, where to park, which entrance to use, what services are available, or whether an online answer is current.

KTS helps businesses provide an accountable source of local information—information that is clear, usable, and approved by the people responsible for the place or service.

KTS does not manufacture attention or generate unverified claims. We help businesses make their real information easier for customers to find, understand, and trust.

## How It Works

1. **Talk.** Tell KTS what customers are getting wrong, missing, or asking about.
2. **Verify.** KTS organizes the relevant information and confirms what the business approves for public use.
3. **Scope.** Receive a written scope, price, timeline, customer responsibilities, and payment terms.
4. **Approve.** Approve the work and pay the required deposit or advance payment.
5. **Launch.** Review completed public information, approve it for release, and receive handoff materials.

## Deposit and Scope Terms

For most projects over $1,000, KTS requires a **50% deposit before work begins** and the remaining 50% before launch or handoff. Small projects may require full payment in advance.

The approved proposal controls the scope, timeline, payment schedule, revisions, customer responsibilities, ownership, maintenance responsibilities, and any third-party costs. Work outside the approved scope requires written approval before additional charges apply.

## Important Expectations

KTS helps customers create clearer, more trustworthy business information, directions, service-area details, QR-linked information hubs, customer-access pages, and maps.

The business remains responsible for the accuracy, legality, permissions, and approval of its public information. KTS will use the information the business provides and approves within the written project scope.

KTS does not promise a specific Google ranking, Google verification, customer traffic, sales result, advertising result, AI-search result, or outcome controlled by Google, an AI system, or another third party.
