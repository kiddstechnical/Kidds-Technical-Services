# Service Packages and Pricing Approach

## Purpose

Kidd's Technical Services, LLC (KTS) provides practical mapping, local-information, digital-preservation, web-support, and ALLIS-related services for small businesses, organizations, and collaborating web firms.

KTS offers simple, understandable entry services for mom-and-pop businesses while also providing specialized technical, mapping, content, and ALLIS implementation support for larger projects. Customers may hire KTS directly, or a website, marketing, design, development, or technology firm may engage KTS as a subcontractor or specialist collaborator.

KTS prices work according to the actual scope, condition of source materials, number of locations, data complexity, access requirements, timeline, customer approvals, required travel, technical risk, and ongoing support needs.

## Pricing Principles

KTS uses a practical, customer-centered pricing approach:

- Small businesses should be able to purchase a defined, useful first service without committing to a large technical project.
- Customers receive a written scope, deliverables, price, payment terms, assumptions, and approval process before work begins.
- Fixed pricing is used when the work is clear and bounded.
- Hourly, day-rate, milestone, or phased-project pricing may be used when the work cannot be accurately priced in advance.
- Third-party costs, including domains, hosting, printing, paid software, hardware, travel, transaction fees, advertising, platform subscriptions, or vendor charges, are identified separately when applicable.
- KTS does not promise a particular Google ranking, traffic level, grant award, funding decision, revenue outcome, or third-party platform result.
- Work beyond the agreed scope requires written approval before KTS begins it.

Prices below are planning ranges and starting points, not binding quotes. KTS confirms pricing through a project-specific proposal or service agreement.

## Small-Business Services

### Get Found Locally

For businesses that need customers to find accurate information, directions, hours, services, and contact options.

Typical deliverables may include:

- Google Business Profile setup, cleanup, or owner-support assistance.
- Review and correction of business name, address, phone number, hours, category, services, website, and map location.
- Addition of customer-provided photos, business description, service details, and contact links.
- QR code linking customers to directions, contact information, booking, ordering, menu, services, or an approved landing page.
- A simple owner guide for updating routine business information.

**Starting range:** $150–$350 for a defined single-location setup or cleanup.

KTS does not guarantee verification approval, search placement, review outcomes, or ranking decisions made by Google, Apple, Bing, social platforms, directories, or other third parties.

### Find Us, Service Area, and Customer Access

For businesses that need a clear way to show customers where they are, where they work, how to park, how to pick up orders, or how to reach them.

Typical deliverables may include:

- A mobile-friendly Find Us, Directions, Parking, Pickup, or Service Area web page.
- Embedded map, approved directions, hours, contact buttons, booking or order links, and accessibility notes provided or approved by the customer.
- Branded service-area, delivery-zone, venue, or customer-access map.
- QR code for signs, cards, tables, receipts, storefronts, vehicles, events, or printed materials.

**Starting range:** $250–$750 for a defined page, map, and QR package.

### Menu, Services, and QR Information Hub

For restaurants, shops, contractors, venues, makers, events, and service providers that need one simple mobile destination for customer information.

Typical deliverables may include:

- A branded one-page information hub.
- Links to menu, services, booking, ordering, products, contact information, directions, social accounts, or customer forms.
- QR code generation and testing.
- Basic map and directions integration.
- Customer-provided or customer-approved photos, branding, and text.

### Event, Venue, and Visitor Map

For events, markets, festivals, reunions, trail events, venues, campgrounds, stores, and community activities.

Typical deliverables may include:

### Local Information Care Plan

For businesses and organizations that need occasional help keeping public information current.

For libraries, archives, museums, historical societies, municipalities, universities, families, churches, and community organizations with maps, records, photographs, reports, or place-based collections.

Typical deliverables may include:

- Collection discovery and inventory.
- Digitization coordination or preparation of customer-supplied digital files.

KTS does not determine legal ownership, copyright status, cultural sensitivity, archival-retention compliance, or public-release eligibility for customer materials. The collection steward remains responsible for rights, permissions, restrictions, and approval decisions.

**Starting range:** $1,000–$5,000.

### ALLIS Node Deployment

For organizations needing a configured ALLIS environment for approved maps, documents, collections, public information, or organization-specific knowledge.

Typical deliverables may include:

### ALLIS Managed Service

For customers that need ongoing technical support, hosting coordination, updates, backups, preservation support, and an agreed operational point of contact.

Possible managed-service components include:

- Hosting or coordinated hosting.
- Routine software and configuration updates.
- Backups and integrity checks.
- Approved content and collection updates.
- Technical support for designated contacts.
- Periodic access, governance, and export review.

**Starting range:** $150–$1,500 per month, depending on the deployment model, number of users, update frequency, hosting, storage, hardware, support expectations, and service-level requirements.

Core public-interest access may be available without a membership charge for eligible government entities, nonprofits, or individuals. Customer-specific discovery, setup, digitization, configuration, customization, integration, hosting, managed operation, training, and support are professional KTS services and may be billed separately.

Examples include:

- Multi-site business or organizational map systems.
- Custom public-information portals.
- Large map and document collections.
- Museum, library, archive, visitor-center, or kiosk deployments.
- Public/private partnership projects.
- Community resource, tourism, heritage, or trail systems.
- Dedicated ALLIS configurations, clustering, integrations, or managed infrastructure.
- Partner-firm implementation agreements.

KTS will prepare a proposal that identifies the planned deliverables, schedule, payment milestones, required customer inputs, third-party costs, maintenance options, ownership and licensing terms, and acceptance process.

## Payment and Engagement Terms

Unless a written agreement states otherwise, KTS may use the following general approach:

- Small defined projects may require payment in full before delivery or a deposit before work begins.
- Larger projects may use a deposit plus milestone payments.
- Managed services are normally billed monthly, quarterly, or annually in advance.
- Customer-requested work outside the agreed scope requires written approval and may be billed separately.
- Third-party costs may require advance payment or direct customer payment.
- KTS may pause work when required approvals, source materials, access, or payments are delayed.

Final pricing, schedule, deliverables, rights, warranties, support commitments, and payment terms are established in the applicable proposal, statement of work, service agreement, or contract.

## Start With the Right Size

KTS begins with a practical question:

> What would make this easier for your customers, staff, visitors, or community to find and use right now?

For a mom-and-pop business, the answer may be correct Google information, a QR page, a service-area map, or a simple directions page. For a website firm, it may be reliable location data, an embedded map, a heritage-map feature, or an interactive visitor tool. For an organization, it may become a larger preservation, public-information, or ALLIS-node project.

KTS can begin with a small, useful service and grow only when the customer’s needs justify a larger engagement.
